# CRA Reporting Obligations

The European Commission page specifying the [Cyber Resilience Act](../concepts/cyber-resilience-act.md) reporting duties that apply **from 11 September 2026**: manufacturers must report **actively exploited vulnerabilities** and **severe incidents** affecting their products with digital elements.

## The timeline (per notifiable event)

| Stage             | Deadline                                                                                                               |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Early warning     | within **24 hours** of becoming aware                                                                                  |
| Full notification | within **72 hours**                                                                                                    |
| Final report      | **14 days** after a corrective measure is available (exploited vulnerabilities); within **1 month** (severe incidents) |

## How and to whom

- Report **once** via the **CRA Single Reporting Platform (SRP)**.
- Notification is addressed to the **[CSIRT](https://csirtsnetwork.eu/)** of the manufacturer's main establishment and, save exceptional circumstances, made available to **[ENISA](../entities/enisa.md)** simultaneously.
- The receiving CSIRT shares the notification without delay with other CSIRTs where the product is available.
- A [delegated act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32026R0881) (adopted 11 Dec 2025) defines when a CSIRT may **delay dissemination** on justified cybersecurity grounds.

## Single Reporting Platform

Under **Article 16**, ENISA is tasked with establishing the SRP; it procured a contractor and the platform will be **operational by 11 September 2026** with a prior testing period. ENISA maintains an [SRP FAQ](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/topics/product-security-and-certification/single-reporting-platform-srp).
