# Cyber Resilience Act — Overview

The European Commission's overview of the [Cyber Resilience Act](../concepts/cyber-resilience-act.md) (CRA), Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 — the EU's horizontal law setting mandatory cybersecurity requirements for **products with digital elements** (hardware + software) placed on the EU market.

## What the CRA does

- Imposes mandatory cybersecurity requirements on **manufacturers** across planning, design, development, and maintenance — at every stage of the value chain.
- Requires manufacturers to **handle vulnerabilities** throughout the product lifecycle.
- Products bear the **CE marking** to show compliance; national market surveillance authorities enforce.
- Some cybersecurity-critical products need **third-party conformity assessment** by a notified body before sale.

## Timeline

- **10 Dec 2024** — entered into force.
- **11 Sep 2026** — [reporting obligations](eu-cra-reporting.md) apply.
- **11 Dec 2027** — main obligations apply.

## Context

Builds on the 2020 EU Cybersecurity Strategy and complements the [NIS2 Directive](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/nis-directive). Key references: [legal text (EU 2024/2847)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32024R2847), summary, and the implementation FAQ. Special approaches exist for [open source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-open-source) and [MSMEs](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-msmes).
