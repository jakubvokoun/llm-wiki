# The EU CRA — Getting Started (ORCWG)

The [Open Regulatory Compliance Working Group](../entities/orcwg.md)'s practical, community-maintained guide to the [Cyber Resilience Act](../concepts/cyber-resilience-act.md), framed for the **open source ecosystem** (maintainers, stewards, vendors).

## Who the CRA affects

- **Manufacturers** — anyone placing a product on the EU market (not limited to EU companies).
- **Open Source Maintainers** — _not_ directly impacted unless they [monetize](https://github.com/orcwg/cra-hub/issues/16) the project; may feel indirect pressure via higher security expectations.
- **Open Source Stewards** — foundations / orgs stewarding (not monetizing) software; a CRA-specific category with its own lighter obligations.

## Why it "changes everything"

Full **supply-chain** compliance was never required before. ~76% of a typical application is open source dependencies, and the CRA reaches the entire chain. [Fines are severe](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj#art_64) and non-compliant products can be pulled from shelves.

## Timeline (as stated by ORCWG)

- 11 Dec 2024 in force; **11 Sep 2026** vulnerability reporting; staged product-category deadlines (type A by 30 Aug 2026; type B/C by 30 Oct 2026); **11 Dec 2027** all obligations.

## ORCWG resources

A neutral forum producing community deliverables — a living [CRA FAQ](https://cra.orcwg.org), an inventory, white papers (SBOMs, manufacturer due diligence, OSS stewards), a **vulnerability management specification**, and numerous formal submissions to EU standards bodies (e.g. contributions to the **CEN/CENELEC vulnerability handling standard**, clause 4.4).
