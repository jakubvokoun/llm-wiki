# Intel

Semiconductor manufacturer; relevant here as an example of a mature, well-documented **[PSIRT](../concepts/psirt.md)** and a **[CNA](../concepts/cve-numbering-authority.md)** that assigns its own CVEs.

## PSIRT process

Three phases — **Identify, Mitigate, Disclose** — using [CVSS](../concepts/cvss.md) base scores, tiered/NDA pre-disclosure, and monthly **Security Advisories** (second Tuesday). See the [process page](../sources/intel-psirt-vulnerability-handling.md).
