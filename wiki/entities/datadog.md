# Datadog

Cloud-based monitoring and observability platform. Provides APM, infrastructure monitoring, log management, security monitoring, and synthetic monitoring in a unified platform. Founded 2010; CNCF ecosystem integrations.

## Products Mentioned

- **Datadog APM** — distributed tracing, service maps, profiling
- **Datadog Monitors** — metric threshold, anomaly, forecast, and composite alerting
- **Datadog Logs** — log ingestion, processing, archiving, search

## Monitoring 101 Series

Datadog's "Monitoring 101" series (2015) remains a widely referenced framework:

- Part 1: Collecting the right data
- Part 2: Alerting on what matters (symptom-based paging, alert severity tiers)
- Part 3: Investigating performance issues
