# ENISA

The **European Union Agency for Cybersecurity**. Provides EU-wide cybersecurity expertise, standards analysis, and operational coordination.

## Role under the CRA

- Tasked (CRA **Article 16**) with establishing and operating the **CRA Single Reporting Platform (SRP)**, live by 11 Sep 2026 — the single channel for [reporting](../sources/eu-cra-reporting.md) actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents.
- Receives CRA notifications (alongside the national [CSIRT](https://csirtsnetwork.eu/)) and publishes guidance (e.g. CRA Requirements↔Standards mapping, SME survey, Secure-by-Design playbook).
