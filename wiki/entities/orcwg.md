# ORCWG

The **Open Regulatory Compliance Working Group** — a neutral, community forum (Eclipse-hosted) helping the open source ecosystem and broader industry achieve [Cyber Resilience Act](../concepts/cyber-resilience-act.md) compliance.

## What it produces

- A living **CRA FAQ** ([cra.orcwg.org](https://cra.orcwg.org)) and inventory.
- White papers (SBOMs, manufacturer due diligence, OSS stewards) and a **vulnerability management specification**.
- Formal submissions to EU bodies — including input to the **CEN/CENELEC vulnerability handling standard** (clause 4.4) and product-category implementing acts.
