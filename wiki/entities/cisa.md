# CISA

The **Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency**, the US federal agency protecting critical infrastructure. Operates national-scale vulnerability coordination and disclosure programs.

## Key functions

- **Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD) program** — synchronizes disclosure across reporters and vendors; reports via **VINCE** (CMU SEI/CERT-CC). May disclose ~45 days after unresponsive-vendor contact.
- **CVE roles** — operates the **CVE Top-Level Root** and is **CNA of Last Resort**, assigning [CVE](../concepts/cve.md) IDs.
- **KEV catalog** — the authoritative list of vulnerabilities **exploited in the wild**, used as a prioritization input.
- Maintains the **VDP Platform** for federal agencies and the OASIS **CSAF** advisory repository.
